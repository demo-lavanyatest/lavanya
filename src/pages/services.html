FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does a website take?

Most Starter and Growth sites launch in 3–6 weeks. Premium projects usually take 6–10 weeks, depending on content and features.

Do I need to provide the content?

You know your business best, so we ask for the key details. Our team writes and polishes the copy and can source professional photos.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We give you a simple way to edit text and images, plus a short training video.

Is hosting included?

We set up fast, secure hosting with SSL in your name. Hosting costs are typically ₹3,000–₹8,000 per year, paid directly to the provider.

What payment terms do you offer?

50% to start, 50% at launch. For Premium projects we can split this into three milestones.
