About us

We help good businesses look great online

A Bengaluru-based team of designers, developers and marketers who care about results.

Our story

Lavanya Digital started in 2015 at a kitchen table, building a website for a friend's bakery. That site tripled her orders, and word spread. Today we are a team of twelve, but we still work the same way: listen first, keep it simple, and measure what matters.

Our mission

To give every small business a website and marketing as good as the big brands, at a fair price.

Our vision

A future where any business in India can compete and win online.
